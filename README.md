# GEE - General Earth Enhancements

A StarMap mod for **Kitten Space Agency** that puts the real world on Earth:
buildings and roads from OpenStreetMap around the camera, city lights at night, and satellite imagery when you look
down from altitude.

> **Heads-up:** this mod was programmed entirely by Claude (Anthropic's AI). It works, but it is a hobby project:
> don't expect anything too special, and expect rough edges.

## Features

- **Buildings and roads** from OpenStreetMap vector tiles, streamed around the camera and cached on disk. Buildings
  get facades, roofs, details nearby and a window pass, and cast shadows in the sun. Roads are laid exactly on the
  terrain, with lane markings, clean junctions, motorway ramps and bridges over the roads below. The game's trees and
  shrubs are cleared from roads and buildings.
- **Far field**: simplified buildings out to tens of kilometres, so cities stay visible after launch.
- **Night**: lit windows, street lights in towns, and city lights visible from orbit.
- **Satellite imagery** (Sentinel-2 cloudless, 2016) draped over the ground when seen from altitude, and the game's
  own terrain coloured from it at every height, so fields, dry ground and forests show near the ground too.
- **Trees where real forests are**: the game's trees, shrubs and grass are kept only where ESA WorldCover land cover
  says they grow, with a few lone trees in open land; none on water, fields, rock or snow.
- **More kinds of trees**: spruces, pines, birches, an autumn poplar and shrubs from a tree pack are mixed into
  the game's own trees.
- **Rocks, bushes, wildflowers and fallen logs** scattered as ground clutter by land cover, slope and noise (rocks on
  steep and bare ground, flower meadows on grassland, bushes near trees, logs in forests); generated by the mod, no third-party assets.
- **Launch complexes** at all of the game's pads on Earth, placed from OpenStreetMap data:
  - **LC-39A** (Kennedy): the fixed service structure (from NASA's "Gantry" model) with a crew access arm that docks
    and undocks, the Starship integration tower, water and lightning towers, hangar, tank farm with boil-off vapour,
    and pipelines. Also LC-39B's towers and the Vehicle Assembly Building.
  - **Starbase** (Boca Chica): both Starship launch towers with their catch arms, and the tank farm.
  - **ELA-4** (Kourou): the Ariane 6 mobile gantry, the four lightning masts and the water tower.
  - **SLC-4E** (Vandenberg): the launch tower and the helium and propellant tanks.
  - **LC-1** (Mahia): the lightning masts and the tank rows of pads A and B.

## Install

1. Install Kitten Space Agency and the StarMap mod loader.
2. Download the zip of the latest release from the
   [Releases page](https://github.com/KarloDerEchte/KSA-GEE-General-Earth-Enhancements/releases) and extract it
   so that you have
   `Documents/My Games/Kitten Space Agency/mods/GEE/` with `mod.toml`, `GEE.dll`, `Assets.xml`, `Trees.xml`, `Scatter.xml`,
   `Meshes/` and `Textures/` in it.
3. Start the game through StarMap. The mod window is in the mods menu.

The mod needs an internet connection the first time an area is shown. Everything downloaded is cached under
`mods/GEE/cache/`, and "Clear download cache" in the window empties it.

## Settings

Every part of the mod can be switched off on its own, in the mod window or in `settings.json` in the mod folder
(written on the first start). For example, turn off the low-poly OSM buildings but keep the roads, the satellite
imagery and the launch complex.

| Setting | Default | What it does |
|---|---|---|
| `Enabled` | on | The whole mod |
| `ShowBuildings` | on | Plain OpenStreetMap buildings (boxes with facades and roofs) |
| `ShowRoads` | on | Roads (and the street light glow along them) |
| `LaunchComplexModels` | on | The modelled launch complexes: LC-39A/B towers, crew arm, VAB, hangar, pipelines, tank farm |
| `Airports` | on | Runways, taxiways, aprons and their lights |
| `Roofs`, `BuildingDetails`, `Windows` | on | Pitched roofs, details near the camera (doors, cornices, roof units), window pass |
| `ReflectorPosts` | on | Small posts along unlit roads |
| `FurnitureDistanceKm` | 3.5 | How far lamp posts and motorway signs/gantries (`RoadFurniture`) are drawn (at least `DetailDistanceKm`); they are culled by their real height, not as 1 m boxes |
| `RoadFurniture` | on | Roadside furniture within `FurnitureDistanceKm`: lamp posts under lit street lamps (also on bridges), an exit sign post 110 m and a cantilever sign gantry 300 m before motorway exits |
| `RoadBarriers` | on | Guard rails along motorway/trunk carriageways, a concrete median barrier where the two carriageways run within 14 m of each other, parapets on bridges. Drawn near the camera (`DetailDistanceKm`, needs `BuildingDetails`), hidden with `ShowRoads`, no collision |
| `NightLights`, `CityGlow`, `RegionalLights`, `GlobalLights` | on | Point lights near the camera, night glow, city lights from orbit |
| `BuildingShadows` | on | Buildings, roofs and tanks cast sun shadows (within the game's 1.5 km shadow range) |
| `ClearClutter` | on | Removes the game's trees and shrubs from roads and buildings |
| `RealTerrain` | off | Real elevation data (about 38 m) in an 80 km window around the camera instead of the game's coarse heightmap; roads, buildings and lakes use it. Needs a restart |
| `WaterReflections` | on | Mirror reflections (sun glint, sky reflection) on the game's water: sea, lagoons and lakes. On (default) the water looks exactly like the unmodified game. Off, the default ocean keeps its colour, depth absorption, refraction, waves and foam but no reflection. Needs a restart |
| `RealisticTrees` | on | Keeps the game's trees, shrubs and grass only where ESA WorldCover says they grow |
| `ForestTreePack` | on | Mixes the tree pack's trees into the game's trees (next game start) |
| `ScatterClutter` | on | Rocks, pebbles, bushes, wildflower patches and fallen logs as ground clutter (next game start; needs `RealisticTrees`) |
| `ScatterDensity` | 1 | Share of them that stay: 0 none, 2 twice as many |
| `LoadRadiusKm`, `RenderDistanceKm` | 3, 15 | How far detailed tiles are loaded and drawn |
| `MaxInstances` | 150000 | Cap on drawn building pieces; beyond it, distant small buildings thin out |
| `FarBuildings`, `FarRadiusKm` | on, 35 | Simplified far buildings |
| `SatelliteImagery`, `SatelliteFadeFromM` | on, 1500 | Satellite imagery from altitude, fading in above this height |
| `GroundColorFromSatellite`, `GroundColorStrength` | on, 0.85 | Colours the game's terrain from the satellite imagery below 8 km, fading out by 20 km where the plain satellite photo takes over (`GroundTintFadeStartKm`/`EndKm`; next game start) |
| `GroundTintFadeStartKm`, `GroundTintFadeEndKm` | 20, 8 | The ground colour fades out from the end altitude (km) up to the start altitude, and the plain satellite photo (as it looked from orbit before the ground colour existed) takes over above the start. 0 = ground colour at every height |
| `GroundTexture`, `GroundTextureStrength`, `GroundTextureNormalStrength`, `GroundTextureScaleM`, `GroundTextureRangeM` | on, 0.8, 0.6, 1, 150 | Small photographed grass/dirt/rock textures inside the terrain shader within the range of the camera: detail and bumps on top of the satellite colour (next game start; needs the ground colour) |
| `StreetLights` | `BuiltUp` | `BuiltUp` (lit in towns, from building density), `Data` (OpenStreetMap lamps, see below), `All`, `None` |
| `StreetLightOverpassUrls` | overpass-api.de | Overpass servers for `Data` mode, comma separated |
| `TankVapor` | on | Vapour over the launch complex tank farms |
| `DebugLogging` | off | Statistics in the window and progress lines in the game's log (errors are always logged); also "Debug" in the GEE menu |

### Street light data (optional)

`StreetLights = "Data"` places street lights where OpenStreetMap maps them (`highway=street_lamp`, `lit=yes/no`).
That data is not in the vector tiles, so it comes from an Overpass API server. The public servers are shared,
volunteer-run and slow (15–90 s per area), and their
[usage policy](https://dev.overpass-api.de/overpass-doc/en/preface/commons.html) asks apps with many users not to
rely on them. The mode is therefore off by default. If you use it, prefer your own Overpass instance; results are
cached for good.

## Building from source

Requirements: .NET 10 SDK, the game, and StarMap.

```
cd src
dotnet build -c Release
```

The build writes `GEE.dll` into the mod folder. If the game is not installed in
`C:\Program Files\Ahwoo\Games\Kitten Space Agency\`, pass `-p:KsaDir="<game folder>\"`. Close the game first: it
locks the DLL.

The meshes and textures are generated by Python scripts (Python 3 with numpy and Pillow):

```
cd src/tools
python make_assets.py
```
