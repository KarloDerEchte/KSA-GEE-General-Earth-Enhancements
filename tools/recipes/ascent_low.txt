# Scripted ascent from the pad to orbit altitude with the flight profiler: free camera climbs 30 m -> 400 km in 180 s
# (exponential, ground track east). Report: profiles/flight_*_ascent.txt; read the slow phases at the end.
load Launch_A
waitidle 60
clouds off
sunel 35
waitidle 40
camera Free
follow Earth
view 28.5 -80.6 300 28.5 -80.0 0
waitidle 60
profile start low
ascend 28.5 -80.6 300 6000 45
profile stop
log ascent done
